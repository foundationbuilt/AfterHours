Common Questions

Answers before you have to ask.

Is the price actually fixed?

Yes — for the build itself. Your tier price covers the pool as designed, and add-ons (spa, heater, chiller, etc.) are priced transparently in the configurator. You see your total before we ever meet.

Can anything change my total?

Site conditions can. If your yard needs demo work, or if the layout requires longer electrical runs, additional excavation access, or specialty equipment to reach the build area, those costs are additional. We identify those on the qualifying call and the site visit — not after you've signed.

Can we modify the pool sizes?

To a degree, yes. If a standard 10×18 won't fit your yard and you need something like 8×20 instead, we can work with the footprint of your space. What we don't do is expand square footage beyond our tier dimensions or build custom shapes. The standardized designs are what let us hold our pricing and timeline.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with lending partners who specialize in pool financing. Details, rates, and application info are on our Financing page.
