Site Requirements
Does Your Backyard Qualify?

Our all-inclusive pricing is built around a straightforward build process. Here's what we need to be true for our pricing to apply:

✓
Relatively flat yard. No slopes or hillsides.
✓
Cleared workspace. Existing grass, pavers, or hardscape needs to be cleared prior to build. Demo and clearing isn't included in our pool pricing. We can quote it separately or you can hire it out. We'll talk through it on the call.
✓
Minimum 4-foot gate access. Our excavation equipment needs room to get in. If your gate is smaller, we'll need to discuss options.
✓
Equipment placement within 40 feet of the waterline. Our equipment pad needs to be within 40 feet of the pool's edge.
✓
Main power panel within 100 feet. Electrical runs beyond 100 feet will require a separate quote.
✓
No hard dig. Some properties have caliche, rock, or other underground conditions that can't be identified until excavation begins. If we hit hard dig, that cost is separate and we'll flag it immediately before proceeding.

Not sure if your yard qualifies? That's exactly what our first call is for. We'll talk through it together.
